The Resolution Library (clean copy)

The Resolution Library was built by Wyatt Ash-Milby at ATNI. It is a workbook that lists ATNI's numbered resolutions with their committee, preparer, convention and outcome.

This folder holds a cleaned copy of that workbook, built on November 23, 2026: 1,724 resolutions from 2000 to 2026. Every value is kept as the workbook writes it, beside a tidied form. The workbook itself was not changed.

Preview, under review. This is not a finished release. Please read these limits before using the numbers.

  • Likely and open. Where the workbook does not say something outright, this copy shows the most likely answer and marks it "likely" in a basis column. Where nothing is likely enough, the value stays as written and is marked "open". There are 269 likely items and 99 open items, all listed in the list to verify for Wyatt Ash-Milby to check.
  • An outcome is recorded for 782 of the 1,724 resolutions, and a preparer for 1,211. A blank means "not recorded". It never means No.
  • A preparer is the name the workbook records in "Prepared By". Spellings of one person's name are joined only where that is likely, and the join is marked: 202 people are listed, 17 of them with a likely join. Names that differ by Jr. or Sr. are never joined without someone who knows.
  • Some committee names changed over the years. An earlier or variant name is counted with its committee as likely (166 links), and every label is kept as written.
  • This is a working list, not ATNI's official record. The resolutions themselves are on the ResIST site and at atnitribes.org.

See the counts as charts in the Analysis preview.
