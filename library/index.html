The Resolution Library (clean copy)

The Resolution Library was built by Wyatt Ash-Milby at ATNI. It is a workbook that lists ATNI's numbered resolutions with their committee, preparer, convention and outcome.

This folder holds a cleaned copy of that workbook, built on November 23, 2026: 1,724 resolutions from 2000 to 2026. Every value is kept as the workbook writes it, beside a tidied form (spacing, number format, file-name titles). The workbook itself was not changed.

Preview, under review. Please read these limits before using the numbers.

  • An outcome is recorded for 781 of the 1,724 resolutions, and a preparer for 1,211. A blank means "not recorded". It never means No.
  • A preparer is the name the workbook records in "Prepared By". Names are shown as written; spellings of one person's name have not been merged.
  • Some committee names changed over the years. Those labels are kept as written and are still being reviewed.
  • This is a working list, not ATNI's official record. The resolutions themselves are on the ResIST site and at atnitribes.org.
